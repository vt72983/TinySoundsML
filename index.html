Ablation Study: CTC Decoding & Correction

Russian speech features complex morphology and case endings that cause standard lightweight CTC greedy decoding to produce high word error rates. Below is our experimental comparison moving from baseline Greedy decoding and Trie search to our dual-assistant pipeline.

Development Path

Roadmap to TinyML Deployment

Transitioning from software algorithmic validation to embedded microcontroller execution.

Stage 1 • Completed / Active

CTC Decoding & Assistants

Built and tested the Russian CTC prototype pipeline. Demonstrated significant WER/CER reduction using prefix trees (Trie) and dual-assistant post-processing on complex Russian phrases.

Stage 2 • In Progress

Acoustic Model Training & QAT

Convergence training of the core CTC acoustic backbone on larger Russian audio datasets, followed by 8-bit integer Quantization-Aware Training (INT8) for memory compression.

Stage 3 • Target Goal

On-Chip MCU Execution

Porting the quantized acoustic graph and lightweight decoder to bare-metal microcontrollers (ESP32-S3 / ARM Cortex-M) for standalone offline speech recognition.
